# Shubi Shot Director v0.9.4

Shubi Shot Director v0.9.4 adds deterministic static actor blocking for the
basic supported poses that previously required Host-authored joint
quaternions or manual correction. It also carries forward the focused-camera
and Windows bridge reliability fixes completed after v0.9.3.

## Deterministic static blocking

- Host Codex may author a narrow `StaticBlockingPlan` with generic body sites,
  support surfaces, broad trunk/leg goals, and relaxed arms. The runtime
  materializes the actor transform and complete canonical pose without prompt
  input or semantic inference.
- Body contacts cover pelvis, upper-back, chest, head, hands, knees, and feet
  against world ground, room floors, planes, and box faces. Diagnostics retain
  terminal gap, penetration, bounds overflow, actor clearance, and body-site
  orientation where anatomy defines a facing direction.
- Relaxed arms now distinguish `free-hanging` from `surface-resting`. A low
  actor may name an optional `restSurface`; the runtime first follows gravity,
  then uses a deterministic two-bone shoulder-elbow-wrist solve when the
  surface stops the hand.
- The surface-resting path preserves canonical elbow bend and joint limits,
  checks the hand contact and complete arm against the surface, and rejects an
  unreachable target instead of forcing an extreme pose.
- `pose inspect` exposes machine-readable joint, contact, orientation, and
  relaxed-limb results. A failed new static solve blocks composition and PNG
  export before visual QA.

## Interaction and runtime maintenance

- Focused camera rotation remains active through real pointer movement and no
  longer stalls behind live runtime events.
- Camera upright control and target-aware editor reframing keep direct camera
  work deterministic without changing the saved editor-camera boundary.
- The Windows bridge helper launches hidden and retains the loopback-only,
  credential-forbidden runtime contract.

## Authority and compatibility

- Application version: 0.9.4.
- SceneSpec, ScenePatch, and IntentReport schema version: 6.
- Capability contract: 2; bridge protocol: 1; workspace routing: 1.
- Existing `actor.pose-joints`, actor height, limb-presence, Blueprint,
  persistence, composition, and PNG export contracts remain available.
- `relaxed-limb.restSurface` is an optional, backward-compatible structured
  extension. Historical schema-valid SceneSpec documents load unchanged and
  retain free-hanging relaxed-arm behavior when the field is absent.
- Static blocking remains deterministic structured materialization. The
  runtime does not accept natural-language prompts, infer aliases, or call a
  model.
- This remains graybox camera previs. v0.9.4 does not add animation, timelines,
  physics, ragdolls, general full-body inverse kinematics, obstacle routing,
  arbitrary mesh editing, final artwork generation, or production asset
  management.

## Verification status

The repeatable gates and dated evidence are recorded in
[`docs/verification.md`](../verification.md#v094-static-blocking-and-resting-arm-gates).
The final `pnpm verify` gate passed 100 regular test files with 2,002 tests, the
separate workspace e2e test (1/1), TypeScript, ESLint, the production Vite
build, and a 305-file public audit with zero findings. The
generic-output audit also completed with zero findings.

Real-browser acceptance used a completely generic ground-resting scene: pelvis
on world ground, upper-back supported by a box, one absent arm chain, bent
resting legs, and the remaining relaxed arm resting on the ground without
manual joint edits. Joint/contact/orientation diagnostics passed, save/load was
stable, and the browser exported a verified 1920 x 1080 Shot Preview PNG. A
side-on negative retained geometric upper-back contact but failed its anatomical
orientation check as required. The conservative composition proxy's support-box
occlusion CHECK was visually inspected in the browser; final visual taste
remains a human approval boundary.
